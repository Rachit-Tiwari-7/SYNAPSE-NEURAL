## 🎯 2. The Core Problem: The 3 Catastrophic Breakdowns of Urban Indian Healthcare

1. **The Delayed Surge Problem (IDSP Lag):** Government hospitals and the Integrated Disease Surveillance Programme (IDSP) learn about outbreaks **7 to 14 days after** transmission has already accelerated exponentially, because they only count patients who physically show up at tertiary hospitals.
2. **The LLM Hallucination Hazard in Pediatrics:** Traditional LLMs routinely hallucinate dangerous adult dosing (recommending 650mg paracetamol tablets to 15kg toddlers) or fail to intercept Aspirin in pediatric viral fever (triggering lethal **Reye's syndrome**).
3. **The Affordability Chasm:** Out-of-pocket pharmaceutical expenses push **55 million Indians below the poverty line every year**, despite the government operating 10,000+ Jan Aushadhi Kendras offering identical generics at up to **90% discount**.

## 🗺️ 6. Municipal Ward Outbreak Heatmap & Predictive $R_t$ Engine

Rather than waiting for hospital bed registries, **The City Immune System** aggregates anonymous symptom signals from citizen WhatsApp triage requests:

- **Syndromic Categorization:** Classifies inputs into Acute Febrile Syndrome (Dengue/Malaria/Chikungunya), Acute Diarrheal Disease (Cholera/Rotavirus), or Acute Respiratory Infection (Influenza/RSV).
- **Reproduction Rate ($R_t$):** Dynamically calculates velocity:
  $$\Delta \text{Signals}_{7d} > 25\% \implies R_t > 1.4 \implies \text{CRITICAL\_SURGE}$$
- **Municipal Action Routing:** Directly dispatches localized ward containment directives (Aedes larviciding, chlorine pipe disinfection, mobile fever clinics) to municipal health officers and ASHA community health workers.
